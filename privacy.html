Privacy Policy

Your mind is a private space. Aether keeps it that way.

Last updated: October 2023

1. Data Collection

If you are using the free Core tier of Aether, all of your data—tasks, habits, journal entries, and goals—is stored locally on your device via SQLite. We do not transmit this data to our servers. Your private thoughts remain exclusively on the hardware you own.

2. Cloud Sync (Pro Tier)

If you opt-in to Aether Pro for cloud syncing, your data is securely transmitted to our Supabase infrastructure. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS, and data at rest is encrypted using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.

3. Analytics

We do not use invasive tracking SDKs. We collect minimal, anonymized telemetry strictly for crash reporting and performance monitoring to ensure the app functions reliably.

4. AI Interactions

When you use the Aether AI assistant, your specific prompt is sent securely to our LLM provider for processing. The provider is not permitted to use your prompts to train their base models. We do not send your entire journal history to the AI unless you explicitly request the AI to analyze a specific entry.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how your data is handled, please reach out to us directly at hello@aetherworld.com.
