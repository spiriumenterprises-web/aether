The War for Attention

Modern software is designed to extract. It extracts your time, your focus, and your attention through endless feeds, red notification dots, and psychological gamification. We are told that to be productive, we need leaderboards, streaks, and artificial urgency.

A Different Approach

Aether was built on a fundamentally different premise: your tools should be quiet. They should get out of your way. Aether does not yell at you when you miss a habit. It does not gamify your life with superficial points. It provides a stark, beautiful canvas for you to project your own intentions onto.
